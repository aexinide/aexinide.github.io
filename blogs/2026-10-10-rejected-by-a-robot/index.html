Rejected by a Robot at 4:19 AM

aexinide · 10/10/2026 · 1 min readtrue storycaptcharant

A true story. Rain, a neighbor's Wi-Fi, a shed full of animals, and one checkbox that apparently had beef with me.

4:19 AM. Bad decisions were made.

It was 4:19 AM. Even the guys who pretend to wake up at 5 to “build their empire” were still asleep. My brain, of course, was wide awake and making terrible plans.

And it was raining. Not drizzle. Angry rain, the kind that sounds like the sky owes somebody money. And my home internet situation was doing absolutely fuck-all. So I walked over to my neighbor's place to borrow the Wi-Fi. Not to study, not to build a startup, not to “grind while everyone sleeps.” You already know where this is going.

And where was I actually?

Inside my neighbor's shed, surrounded by goats, buffaloes, cows and an ox. also some chicken

Fucking hell.

Why there? Because I have no network at home, so I walked through mud and rain to borrow my neighbor's Wi-Fi. Meanwhile, somewhere out there, normal people were asleep in warm beds, dreaming about their futures and their beautiful lives. And I was in a goddamn animal shed, trying to get online.

I connected to the network, opened Chrome, and typed in the address Pornhub.com

Enter. Then I waited.

But no.

I'm not a robot

Instead of the page I wanted, I got a CAPTCHA. A little checkbox asking me to confirm I wasn’t a robot.

WHAT THE ACTUAL FUCK?

Bro, just look around. I'm sitting in a shed with a fucking buffalo breathing somewhere behind me. It’s raining. The goats are having a better night than I am. And you’re asking me to prove I’m not a robot?

I stared at that screen like someone who just got dumped over text.

Try again

I clicked it. Nothing useful happened. I tried again.

Again. And again. Same damn message. At this point, I was arguing with a checkbox like it owed me money.

Motherfucker.

WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU WANT FROM ME?

My patience was dying faster than my phone battery. I was standing in rain-soaked darkness, surrounded by animals who had zero interest in my suffering, fighting a machine that didn’t even know I existed.

And then came the second problem. My dick was getting harder, my patience was getting shorter, and this piece of shit website kept asking me to prove I’m not a robot. Imagine the irony. My body was providing more evidence of my humanity than the website could ever process, and it still wasn’t convinced.

I wasn’t hacking NASA. I wasn’t running a cyberattack. I wasn’t stealing anyone’s data. I was just a horny human being trying to have a private moment before sunrise. And apparently even that now requires technical qualifications.

Try again? TRY AGAIN AT WHAT, YOU DIGITAL BASTARD?

The shed

By now, I was soaked. I was sitting in my neighbor's shed upside-down bucket with goats, buffaloes, cows and an ox. The smell was strong. The rain on the roof was louder than my own thoughts, and my phone was the only light in the whole place.

And the fear of if someone catch me in a shed like this at 4 am.

One goat stared straight at me like, “Bro, what the fuck are you doing here at 4:19 AM?” Fair question.

The buffalo kept chewing, completely unbothered. The cow was definitely judging my life decisions. The ox just existed, as always.

Not one of them has ever had to prove it’s an animal. They were living their lives. I was the only idiot in the shed arguing with a fucking CAPTCHA.

My Inner Monologue. Am I a robot? Let me check

So I just stand up and move my hand inside the pant to check am i actually a human? yes ofc! i am human, coz i have dick and have feelings.

And yet this website thinks I’m a robot? What kind of robot has a dick to masturbate? What kind of robot sits in a goat shed in the rain, horny and furious, fighting a checkbox? If I were a robot I’d at least have better Wi-Fi.

The real question

Look, I get it. Websites use CAPTCHA to stop bots, spam and malicious traffic. But this wasn’t a cybersecurity battle. This was a personal attack.

Then another thought hit me. Why does a robot need to masterbate? Does he have dick to playing by hand to get pleasure? If an actual robot wanted to watch porn, why the fuck is that my problem? Is it lonely? And if it does, does it also have to click on traffic lights before it can experience whatever the hell a robot considers pleasure?

Someone needs to explain this joke to me.

The ending

After twenty minutes of clicking, failing and cursing, I gave up. I closed Chrome and sat there in the shed, listening to the rain, watching the animals breathe, and wondering how the fuck we got here.

Human has sent rockets to space, mapped the human genes, built AI and created machines that that solves complex problems. And at 4:19 AM, in the rain, I was defeated by a fucking checkbox.

Maybe the site just glitched. Maybe my network was just bad. Maybe I should’ve gone back to sleep like a normal person.

Or maybe God gave me hint that i have birth to do something big!

But right then I didn’t feel intelligent, technologically advanced even i have studied computer science. I just felt like a dumb human being.
A horny, rain-soaked, sleep-deprived human being.

And the buffalo, honestly, looked like it understood. Or maybe it was just chewing grass. Either way, it was having a better night than me.

And I leave there and go home and then sleep till the night.
